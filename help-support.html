Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book an appointment?

Open Find Doctors, select a doctor and follow the appointment booking steps.

How can I view my medical reports?

Open Medical Reports from your dashboard. Your available reports will appear there.

Can I consult a doctor online?

Online consultation requires a supported video consultation feature and a confirmed appointment.

How can I update my profile?

Open My Profile to manage your personal details.

What if I forget my password?

Open the login page and select Forgot Password to access the password recovery process.
